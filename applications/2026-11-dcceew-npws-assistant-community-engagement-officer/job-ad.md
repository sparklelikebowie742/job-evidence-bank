National Parks and Wildlife Service has a unique opportunity for an Assistant Community Engagement Officer to work as part of the team and contribute towards the conservation of the natural environment, flora, fauna and Aboriginal heritage.

This is an internal-facing role. Unlike many community engagement positions, you won't be spending your time facilitating public meetings or consulting directly with external communities. Instead, you'll work behind the scenes with NPWS staff across NSW to develop resources, communications and engagement programs that support conservation, visitor experience and community outcomes.

You'll build relationships with a diverse range of internal stakeholders, helping project teams deliver effective engagement activities and communications. Success in this role comes from collaboration, strong written communication and the ability to quickly adapt to new projects and priorities.

The role combines interesting, high-profile project work with routine but essential tasks such as maintaining stakeholder databases, preparing email communications, coordinating project activities and updating systems. We're looking for someone who sees value in both and who is happy to roll up their sleeves, tackle the everyday essentials and then pivot to new and interesting challenges when they arise.

### Who we are

The Department of Climate Change, Energy, the Environment and Water (DCCEEW) works to protect the state's environment and heritage. It leads the way on climate change, driving the sustainable transition to a net zero economy, powered by affordable, reliable, and clean energy.

DCCEEW conserves and protects the state's natural environment. It manages the NSW national park estate, including its rich and diverse biodiversity and Aboriginal cultural heritage for future generations.

DCCEEW also ensures sustainable management of water resources across the state, to support the environment, communities and industry.

We acknowledge the ongoing custodial responsibilities of the Aboriginal peoples of NSW to care for Country and water and are committed to establishing meaningful partnerships with Aboriginal peoples in the management of the environment.
