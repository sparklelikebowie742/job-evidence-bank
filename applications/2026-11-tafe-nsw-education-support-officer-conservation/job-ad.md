**About the role**

As an Education Support Officer, you will support teachers and build engagement with learners studying Conservation & Ecosystem Management courses. This role involves supporting online course delivery while working alongside teachers during in-person workshops. You will have the opportunity to apply your on-the-job knowledge and industry specific qualification to create a positive learning environment. You will supplement the teaching activities by providing follow-up instruction as provided by a teacher and opportunities to practice skills to enhance students educative experience and engagement.

**About You**

You are a confident and adaptable communicator with strong interpersonal skills. You can explain concepts clearly, motivate students and build effective working relationships. You are comfortable using Microsoft Office applications, including MS Teams, and are willing to learn new systems or platforms to support student needs.

To succeed in this role, you will:
- Have appropriate subject knowledge, skills, industry experience and hold a Certificate III Conservation and Ecosystem Management or equivalent.
- Highly Desirable to also hold a Certificate III Horticulture or relevant industry experience.
- Have demonstrated strong administrative skills, and experience.
- Have high-level interpersonal and communication skills, including your ability to be proactive and show initiative.
- Have skills in the use of relevant information and communication technologies.
- Be able to meet all the selection criteria in the below role description.
