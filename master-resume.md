## Professional summary

Ecologist and council bushland field officer with nearly three years delivering bush regeneration, revegetation and threatened species recovery across NSW. I've managed bush regeneration across 42 reserves (about 3,000 ha), including 93 ha of threatened ecological communities, and planted 20,000+ native plants in a year at 80% survival. I now co-manage two $20,000 restoration projects at Northern Beaches Council.

I plan works around the seasons and site conditions, supervise and train Bushcare volunteers (120+ working bees a year), and work with Local Land Services, Saving our Species, Aboriginal Elders, the RFS, contractors and residents. I use ArcGIS, Field Maps, Survey123 and BioNet to map works and record threatened species. Before moving into the field, I was a consultant at Nous Group, where I co-designed a framework for the NSW Government's Conservation and Threatened Species functions. I hold First Class Honours in Wildlife Conservation and Management and a first-author paper in Austral Entomology.
