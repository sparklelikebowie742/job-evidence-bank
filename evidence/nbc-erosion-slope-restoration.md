# Erosion control and revegetation on a steep residential slope

- **Employer / dates:** Northern Beaches Council, Jun 2026 – present (ongoing)
- **NSW capabilities (rough level):** Plan and Prioritise (Adept); Deliver Results (Intermediate–Adept); Project Management (Intermediate); Commit to Customer Service (Intermediate); Work Collaboratively (Intermediate)
- **Skills / keywords:** erosion control, coir logs, jute mat and netting, revegetation, primary weed control, vine control, woody weeds, herbicide application, power tools, watering regime, monitoring, project budget, co-managing projects, resident/community liaison

## Situation
A heavily eroded slope of about 4,000 sqm between residential blocks on the Northern Beaches, infested with vines and woody weeds. An earlier attempt by a previous team (different manager and staff) had been abandoned, and residents had wanted the area restored for years. Budget: $20,000.

## Task
Co-manage the project with a colleague, from site assessment and restoration strategy through to planting and aftercare, including choosing species and materials and working within the budget.

## Action
- Installed coir logs, jute mat and jute netting to stabilise the slope and stop soil loss.
- Carried out primary weed control: herbicide spraying for the vine infestation and power tool work on woody weeds to prepare the area for planting.
- Planted 1,200+ native grasses along the slope, using planting technique suited to stabilising an eroding slope.
- Set up ongoing follow-up spraying and a watering regime to protect the planting, and monitor the site continuously.

## Result
- 1,200+ grasses established and surviving well.
- No wash-outs after rain, thanks to the erosion control and planting technique.
- Turned around a site a previous team had abandoned.
- Regular positive feedback from residents, who said they'd wanted the area restored for years.
- Manager highly satisfied; gave positive feedback and recognition through business unit meetings.
