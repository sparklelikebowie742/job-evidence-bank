# Organising speakers for the AABR 40/40 Forum (40th anniversary)

- **Employer / dates:** Australian Association of Bush Regenerators (AABR), volunteer working group, 2026 (about 3 months, leading up to the forum on 27 November 2026)
- **NSW capabilities (rough level):** Plan and Prioritise (Adept); Work Collaboratively (Adept); Communicate Effectively (Intermediate–Adept); Display Resilience and Courage (Intermediate); Commit to Customer Service (Intermediate); Technology (Intermediate)
- **Skills / keywords:** event coordination, forum, guest speakers, presenter liaison, stakeholder engagement, professional networks, run sheet, program, Slido, audience Q&A moderation, Excel, Google Drive, registrations, purchasing, volunteer working group, bush regeneration sector, AABR, hybrid event

## Situation
AABR, the peak body for bush regenerators in Australia, is holding the AABR 40/40 Forum on 27 November 2026 in Surry Hills to celebrate its 40th anniversary. It's a hybrid event (in person and online) for up to 400 attendees, with 14 speakers. It's being organised by a working group of 8 people, including paid AABR staff, and the main organiser has a heavy workload.

## Task
As a volunteer member of the working group, coordinate the guest speakers and presenters, and help with forum admin and logistics.

## Action
- Attended weekly working group meetings over about 3 months (more often as the deadline got closer), putting in about 6 hours a week on average.
- Sourced guest speakers through my own professional network and the wider working group's network.
- Liaised with all 14 speakers and presenters: drafted invitation and briefing emails, notified them of arrangements, collected their bios, slides and abstracts by deadline, and identified the materials each one needed.
- Coordinated late-confirmed presenters by email to get their details in on time.
- Built and updated the forum program and run sheet, and set up Slido for audience questions.
- Handled registrations and attendee questions.
- Purchased and organised materials and gifts for presenters.
- Kept speaker, logistics and admin information organised in Excel and Google Drive so the whole group could work from it.
- Noticed small tasks that had been missed or not accounted for because of the main organiser's workload, and picked them up without being asked.
- On the day, I'll be running timing to keep sessions on schedule, supporting presenters, and helping moderate audience questions in the room.

## Result
- 14 speakers confirmed and coordinated for a hybrid forum of up to 400 attendees.
- Program, run sheet and Slido ready for the day.
- My work on the group led to an invitation to join the AABR Board.
