# Threatened fauna surveys: koalas and Glossy Black Cockatoos

- **Employer / dates:** Volunteer/field surveyor, 2025–2026 (Glossies in the Mist, Saving our Species, 2025; Koala Sentinel Monitoring Program, NSW Government, 2025–2026)
- **NSW capabilities (rough level):** Deliver Results (Intermediate); Technology (Intermediate); Work Collaboratively (Intermediate)
- **Skills / keywords:** fauna survey, threatened species, koala, Glossy Black Cockatoo, Saving our Species, GPS navigation, spotlighting, drone detection, transect surveys, feed trees, chewed cones, NatureMapr, data recording

## Situation
NSW Government monitoring programs needed field surveyors to confirm threatened species on the ground.

## Task
Survey for koalas and Glossy Black Cockatoos and record observations accurately.

## Action
- Koala Sentinel Monitoring Program (2 annual surveys): navigated by GPS to drone-detected koalas, spotlighted, and recorded observations.
- Glossies in the Mist (1 survey): ran transect surveys to locate feed trees and feeding evidence (chewed cones), and submitted records to NatureMapr.

## Result
- 8 koalas found on each of the 2 annual surveys.
- 3 Glossy Black Cockatoos found, plus feeding evidence recorded.
