## Situation
On-ground bush regeneration relies on a steady set of everyday tasks being done well: collecting seed, mulching, replacing failed or missing plants, removing rubbish, keeping tools and vehicles safe, and keeping accurate records.

## Task
Do these tasks reliably and safely as part of council and contract restoration works.

## Action
- **Seed and propagation (Northern Beaches Council):** collect local provenance seed from 20+ species, and help council-run nurseries propagate the species needed for local plantings.
- **Mulching:** mulch for weed suppression and around plantings.
- **Replacement planting:** replant areas with poor canopy, mid-storey and groundcover.
- **Rubbish removal:** remove rubbish from bushland reserves.
- **Plant and equipment:** do pre-start checks and maintain all the power tools I use; tow trailers; drive a manual ute, with off-road experience.
- **Records:** keep works logs, work schedules, chemical application logs and incident reports, and kept Bushcare attendance records at Wingecarribee.
- **Workplace training:** completed training at both councils in EEO, code of conduct, diversity and cultural awareness, and conflict resolution and management.

## Result
- Reliable, safe delivery of the everyday restoration tasks council bush regeneration roles ask for.
