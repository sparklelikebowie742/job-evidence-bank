# Training staff and volunteers, and explaining science to different audiences

- **Employer / dates:** Wingecarribee Shire Council (Dec 2024 – May 2026), Northern Beaches Council (Jun 2026 – present), plus earlier jobs and study
- **NSW capabilities (rough level):** Communicate Effectively (Adept); Manage and Develop People (Intermediate); Value Diversity and Inclusion (Intermediate); Technology (Intermediate)
- **Skills / keywords:** training, on-the-job instruction, tutoring, mentoring, work experience student, adult learning, tailored instruction, weed ID, herbicide methods, mapping tools, council software, presentations, info sheets, biosecurity, plain language, Moodle, MS Teams, blended learning

## Situation
In every job I've had, new staff and volunteers needed to learn practical skills quickly and safely. I also often need to explain scientific and environmental ideas to people with very different backgrounds, from academics to landholders.

## Task
Train new people so they could work safely and independently, and explain complex ideas clearly to each audience.

## Action
- Trained 10+ new staff across my jobs. At Wingecarribee I trained 2 new team members and 15 volunteers in:
  - weed identification
  - herbicide methods
  - mapping tools and council software
  - scheduling works
- Supervised a work experience student at Northern Beaches Council.
- Tailored training to each person's pace and learning style.
- Presented high-level scientific ideas to scientists, academics, professors, teachers and students.
- Wrote guides and info sheets for landholders and other non-specialists on environmental issues and biosecurity.
- Use MS Teams every day at work for communication, documents, databases, scheduling and presentations. I've used Moodle for about 12 years as a school, TAFE and university student.
- Studied my Cert III in Conservation and Ecosystem Management at Ryde TAFE in blended mode. I learnt best with a solid theory base followed straight away by field practice, regular follow-up from teachers, and a mix of visual guides, lectures and fieldwork.

## Result
- 10+ staff trained across my jobs, including 2 new team members and 15 volunteers at Wingecarribee.
- Received strong positive feedback on three occasions for my tailored training, positive teaching style and approachability.
- Excellent feedback and high marks for my scientific presentations.
