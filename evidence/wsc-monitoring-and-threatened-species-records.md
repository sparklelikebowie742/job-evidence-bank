# Vegetation monitoring and threatened species records

- **Employer / dates:** Wingecarribee Shire Council, Dec 2024 – May 2026
- **NSW capabilities (rough level):** Think and Solve Problems (Intermediate); Technology (Intermediate–Adept); Demonstrate Accountability (Intermediate); Deliver Results (Intermediate)
- **Skills / keywords:** vegetation monitoring, Biodiversity Assessment Method (BAM), monitoring plots, vegetation condition assessment, BioNet, threatened species records, Brachyscome mittagongensis, data management, long-term monitoring

## Situation
Council's in-house bush regeneration team had started long-term monitoring to measure how well revegetation and bush regeneration were working.

## Task
Collect before-and-after vegetation data and record threatened species found during works.

## Action
- Set up and surveyed 4 monitoring plots based on the Biodiversity Assessment Method (as a non-accredited assessor, with adapted plot sizes), plus vegetation condition assessments before and after works.
- Added the data to council records for the long-term monitoring program.
- Recorded threatened species sightings to BioNet, including the Endangered daisy Brachyscome mittagongensis.

## Result
- 4 BAM-based plots established and feeding council's long-term monitoring program.
- Threatened species records added to the state database (BioNet).
