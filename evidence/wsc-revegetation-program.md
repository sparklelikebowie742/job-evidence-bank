# Revegetation program: 20,000+ plants at 80% survival

- **Employer / dates:** Wingecarribee Shire Council, Dec 2024 – May 2026
- **NSW capabilities (rough level):** Deliver Results (Adept); Plan and Prioritise (Intermediate–Adept); Project Management (Intermediate); Work Collaboratively (Intermediate); Communicate Effectively (Intermediate)
- **Skills / keywords:** revegetation, planting, Robertson Rainforest, threatened ecological community, survival monitoring, watering regime, follow-up, community planting, school planting, volunteers, project delivery

## Situation
Council needed to revegetate degraded bushland across its reserves, including the threatened ecological community Robertson Rainforest.

## Task
Lead or co-lead revegetation projects: planning, planting, coordinating volunteers and community groups, and making sure plants survived.

## Action
- Led or co-led 10+ revegetation projects in one year.
- The largest was 3,600 sqm of Robertson Rainforest, planted by the in-house team and, on some occasions, with local public school and high school students, community groups and volunteers.
- Ran follow-up visits every two weeks after planting, kept up regular watering, and counted dead plants to track survival.

## Result
- 20,000+ native plants planted in one year.
- 80% survival at assessment.
- Restored 3,600 sqm of a threatened ecological community.
