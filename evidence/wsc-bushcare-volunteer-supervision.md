# Supervising Bushcare volunteers: 120+ working bees a year

- **Employer / dates:** Wingecarribee Shire Council, Dec 2024 – May 2026
- **NSW capabilities (rough level):** Manage and Develop People (Intermediate); Communicate Effectively (Adept); Value Diversity and Inclusion (Adept); Demonstrate Accountability (Adept, WHS); Work Collaboratively (Adept)
- **Skills / keywords:** Bushcare, supervising volunteers, volunteer coordination, safety inductions, toolbox talks, emergency procedures, first aid, incident reporting, training, community engagement, inclusive practice, volunteer emails, contact lists, attendance records, stakeholder database, enterprise council software

## Situation
Council's Bushcare program had up to 350 active volunteers, of different ages, language backgrounds and fitness levels, working in council bushland.

## Task
Independently supervise Bushcare working bees and keep volunteers safe, productive and coming back.

## Action
- Supervised 10+ working bees a month, with up to 15 volunteers each.
- Opened each session with a toolbox and safety talk, emergency procedures and the work plan, and set out work areas for volunteers.
- Ran safety inductions and trained new volunteers.
- Emailed volunteers to keep them informed about working bees.
- Kept detailed volunteer contact lists and attendance records in council's enterprise software.
- Adjusted tasks and explanations for different ages, language backgrounds and fitness levels.
- As a trained first aider, gave first aid when needed, reported incidents immediately in council software, and followed up with volunteers afterwards.

## Result
- 120+ working bees a year, supervised independently.
- Supported a program of up to 350 active volunteers.
- Incidents managed and reported promptly, with volunteers followed up.
