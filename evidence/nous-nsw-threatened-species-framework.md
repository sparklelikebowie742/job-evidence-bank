# Co-designing a framework for NSW Government Conservation and Threatened Species functions

- **Employer / dates:** Nous Group, Mar – Oct 2023
- **NSW capabilities (rough level):** Think and Solve Problems (Adept); Communicate Effectively (Adept); Work Collaboratively (Intermediate–Adept); Commit to Customer Service (Intermediate); Plan and Prioritise (Intermediate)
- **Skills / keywords:** NSW Government, Environment and Heritage, threatened species, conservation policy, stakeholder consultation, research and analysis, synthesis, framework design, reporting, presentations, client engagement

## Situation
NSW Government (Environment and Heritage) engaged Nous Group to help overhaul its Conservation and Threatened Species functions.

## Task
As a graduate consultant working alongside senior consultants, help design a new framework from stakeholder input.

## Action
- Ran stakeholder consultation and synthesised notes into findings.
- Did research and analysis, and helped create the framework and solutions.
- Built slide decks to visualise findings and solutions.
- Reported results to government clients, engaging them throughout delivery to align objectives and gather feedback.
- Met timelines across concurrent engagements.

## Result
- Co-designed a framework that a separate delivery team later implemented.
