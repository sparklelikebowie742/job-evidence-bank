# Staged primary weed control on a 10,000 sqm urban site

- **Employer / dates:** Northern Beaches Council, Jun 2026 – present (ongoing)
- **NSW capabilities (rough level):** Plan and Prioritise (Adept); Think and Solve Problems (Intermediate); Project Management (Intermediate); Deliver Results (Intermediate)
- **Skills / keywords:** primary weed control, vine control, woody weeds, Ipomoea, Madeira vine, lantana, ochna, privet, senna, Arundo donax, coral tree, chainsaw, power tools, cut-and-paint, stem injection, knapsack spraying, selective and non-selective herbicide, staged works, revegetation planning, project budget

## Situation
A 10,000 sqm urban bushland site on the Northern Beaches heavily infested with vines and woody weeds: Ipomoea, Madeira vine, lantana, ochna, privet, senna, Arundo donax and coral trees. Budget: $20,000.

## Task
Co-manage, with a colleague, staged primary weed control and revegetation works to bring the site back to a condition where it can be replanted.

## Action
- Planned the works in stages so each cleared area could be followed up before moving on.
- Matched the control method to each weed: chainsaws and power tools for large woody weeds, manual removal, cut-and-paint, stem injection, and knapsack spraying with selective and non-selective herbicides.

## Result
- 800 sqm through primary clearing so far (ongoing).
