- **NSW capabilities (rough level):** Demonstrate Accountability (Adept); Act with Integrity (Intermediate); Communicate Effectively (Intermediate)
- **Skills / keywords:** WHS, toolbox talks, risk identification, hazard control, chemical records, chemical storage, AQF3 chemical accreditation, incident reporting, SolvSafety, first aid, chainsaw, asset protection zones, safe work

## Situation
Bush regeneration involves herbicides, chainsaws and power tools, steep and uneven ground, and working with volunteers and the public.

## Task
Keep myself, my team, volunteers and the public safe, and meet council and contract WHS standards.

## Action
- Ran toolbox talks and safety briefings for team members and volunteers.
- Identified risks and put controls in place before works.
- Kept accurate chemical application records and stored chemicals safely.
- Reported incidents promptly (in SolvSafety at Northern Beaches) and followed up with the people involved.
- Gave first aid when needed.
- Cleared asset protection zones to contract scope and WHS standards (Total Earth Care).

## Result
- Incidents reported promptly and the people involved followed up.
- Chemical records kept accurately and chemicals stored safely, in line with AQF3 chemical accreditation.
- Asset protection zones cleared to contract scope and WHS standards.
