# Community planting days and landholder weed education with Local Land Services

- **Employer / dates:** Wingecarribee Shire Council, Dec 2024 – May 2026
- **NSW capabilities (rough level):** Communicate Effectively (Adept); Commit to Customer Service (Adept); Work Collaboratively (Adept); Influence and Negotiate (Intermediate); Value Diversity and Inclusion (Intermediate)
- **Skills / keywords:** community engagement, landholder engagement, Local Land Services, weed education, priority weeds, Biosecurity Act, school planting, community planting, public events, plain-language communication, stakeholder partnerships

## Situation
Weeds on private land spread into council bushland, and many landholders didn't know their obligations for priority weeds. Council also wanted schools and the community involved in restoring local bushland.

## Task
Represent council at public weed education events run with Local Land Services, and run community and school planting events.

## Action
- Represented council at 4 weed education events with Local Land Services, each with up to 60 landholders, advising on priority weed identification and management in plain language.
- Ran planting events with 3 schools and 10 community events, with up to 50 participants each.
- Gave planting demonstrations for walk-in participants, tailored to different ages, language backgrounds and fitness levels.

## Result
- Reached up to about 240 landholders across 4 Local Land Services events.
- Delivered 13 school and community planting events.
- Strengthened council's working relationship with Local Land Services.
