# Propagating Endangered Camden Woollybutt and running the community nursery

- **Employer / dates:** Wingecarribee Shire Council, Dec 2024 – May 2026
- **NSW capabilities (rough level):** Deliver Results (Adept); Display Resilience and Courage (Adept); Manage Self (Adept); Work Collaboratively (Intermediate); Commit to Customer Service (Intermediate)
- **Skills / keywords:** threatened species recovery, Saving our Species, Eucalyptus macarthurii, Endangered species, nursery propagation, provenance seed, seed collection and storage, plant health, supervising nursery volunteers, Land for Wildlife, internal customers

## Situation
Eucalyptus macarthurii (Camden Woollybutt) is Endangered and endemic to the Southern Highlands. Saving our Species supplied wild-sourced seed for council to grow and plant. The council's community nursery also supplied plants for council revegetation, Saving our Species projects and Land for Wildlife giveaways to private landholders. Partway through, the experienced nursery officer went on secondment for six months.

## Task
Propagate and establish Camden Woollybutt, fill plant orders from council's tree teams and other colleagues, and keep the nursery running while the nursery officer was away.

## Action
- Worked with the in-house bush regeneration team to propagate, plant, and coordinate plantings with volunteers, community groups and schools.
- Took over nursery operations for six months and learnt the role within one week.
- Grew provenance stock from seed, stored seed correctly, and monitored plants for disease.
- Supervised the nursery Bushcare volunteer groups.
- Propagated 50+ native species to fill orders from tree teams and other council environmental projects.

## Result
- 2,000+ Camden Woollybutt planted in one year in council bushland across the Southern Highlands, where the species is endemic.
- Kept the nursery producing high-quality provenance stock for six months without its usual officer.
- Excellent feedback from my manager.
- Supplied council revegetation, Saving our Species projects and Land for Wildlife giveaways.
